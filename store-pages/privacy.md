# Privacy Overview

Last updated: {{LAST_UPDATED}}

This page explains the storefront’s observable data flows. The merchant has not yet supplied verified controller identity, contact details, retention periods, or jurisdiction-specific legal bases. Those items must be completed by the merchant for a full privacy notice.

## Data used by the storefront

- Catalogue pages load public product, category, image, price, stock, and article information from Selldone.
- The browser stores the shopping bag and selected visual theme locally on the customer’s device.
- Customer sign-in uses Selldone OAuth with Authorization Code and PKCE. This storefront does not contain a client secret.
- Checkout, payment, order history, and customer profile data are handled through the customer’s authenticated Selldone session.
- Product and basket records can include the responsible marketplace vendor so the correct seller can fulfil and support the order.
- A newsletter submission sends the entered email address to the shop’s Selldone audience stream.

## Payment and fulfilment

Enabled payment providers and delivery services may receive the information necessary to process an order. The final checkout identifies the methods available for that transaction. This storefront does not store full payment-card details.

## Local storage

Essential local storage keeps the bag, sign-in tokens, OAuth state, and theme preference. Clearing site data removes those browser-side values and may sign the customer out.

## Customer choices

Customers can sign out from the account panel and can clear browser data through their browser. Requests concerning an order or account should be made through the authenticated Selldone customer area until the merchant publishes a verified support address.

## Merchant action required

Before collecting customer orders at scale, the merchant should publish its legal identity, privacy contact, processing purposes, legal bases, provider list, international-transfer safeguards, retention periods, and the rights available in each served market.
