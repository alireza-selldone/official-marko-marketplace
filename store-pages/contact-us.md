# Contact Marko

## Order help

For an existing order, sign in with the Selldone customer account used at checkout and open the order record. This keeps the request connected to the correct customer and transaction.

Include the order number, the product and selected variant, and a concise description of the question. If an item appears damaged or different from the listing, keep the packaging and attach clear photographs through the available order-support flow.

## Product questions

Product pages show the currently available colors, sizes, stock, price, and published specifications. When material, care, fit, or measurements are not shown, do not infer them from the photograph; ask the merchant for product-specific confirmation before ordering.

Once verified details are available, they should be added here and to the Selldone business profile. Until then, use the authenticated account and order tools for transaction-related support.
